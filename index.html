About the Foundation

Building Opportunities Through Education & Empowerment

The Dr. C.N. Ashwathnarayan Foundation works alongside schools, teachers and communities to create meaningful opportunities — especially for those who have had the fewest.

Our work spans education, technology, youth development and community welfare, with one simple belief: every young person deserves the tools to shape their own future.

Featured Initiative

Technology Transforming Education

STEP — Sikshana Technology in Education Program

STEP brings technology-enabled learning into government schools — so that a child's classroom, not their circumstances, decides what they can learn.

  • Digital classrooms in government schools
  • Curriculum-aligned educational content
  • Technology resources and support for teachers
